## How It Works (3 Steps for a 70-Year-Old)

**Step 1: Send Photos of Your Dog**
You upload 5-10 pictures of Bailey — a close-up, a few side views, maybe one of him playing. That's all we need to know what he looks like.

**Step 2: We Create the Episode**
Our artists make a Pixar-style cartoon adventure where Bailey is the star. He goes on a quest, makes friends, solves a problem. Each episode is about a minute of continuous cartoon, built to loop seamlessly for as long as Bailey wants to watch. You get it in your inbox in about a week.

**Step 3: It Appears on Your TV**
The episode shows up on a YouTube channel we set up just for you. Turn on your TV, find the channel, hit play. No passwords to remember. No confusing menus. Just Bailey's adventure, ready to watch.

## FAQ

**Q: Is it really my dog in the cartoon?**
A: Yes. We use the photos you upload. Our artists study the shots and build Bailey to look like him—his color, markings, size, the way he stands. It's not just any dog. It's Bailey.

**Q: How does it get on my TV?**
A: We set up a YouTube channel for you. You watch it on any TV that plays YouTube—Roku, Apple TV, smart TV, even your computer plugged into the screen. No apps to install. No setup. Just a link and a bookmark.

**Q: What if the cartoon doesn't look right?**
A: Tell us. Describe what's off—"his tail curls up" or "he's got a white spot on his chest." We regenerate the episode and send you the fixed version. No extra charge. We want Bailey to look like Bailey.

**Q: Is my info private? What about photos?**
A: Your photos stay yours. We use them to create your episode. We don't sell them. We don't share them. We don't train models on them. You own the photos; we just borrow them to make the cartoon. When your subscription ends, we delete our copies.

**Q: What if I don't like it? Can I get my money back?**
A: Thirty-day refund. No questions. If the cartoon doesn't work for you, we'll refund the whole thing. Just email us.

**Q: How long does it take to get an episode?**
A: About a week. You upload photos on Monday, your episode is usually ready by Friday. Sometimes faster. We'll email you when it's live.

**Q: Do I need YouTube Premium? Will there be ads?**
A: You don't need premium. The channel is free to watch. There may be ads on some videos—YouTube adds them sometimes. Our season pass is designed to minimize interruptions so you and Bailey get quality time together. Single episodes may have more ad placement.

**Q: What about cats? Can I make a cartoon with my cat?**
A: Not yet. Dogs first. But cats are coming soon. If you want to sign up for the cat launch, we'll add you to the list.
